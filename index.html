Philosophical Architecture

HomoDynamics derives its grammar from old intuitions—flux, breath, interdependence, balance, time, selfhood, ethics, attention—translated into the language of dynamics.

Flux → Process over Substance

Heraclitus’ panta rhei, Buddhist impermanence, Daoist verbs: stability belongs to behavior across time, not to a frozen identity.

Breath/Energy → Order far from Equilibrium

Vedic prāṇa, Chinese qì: life as throughput—capture, transform, distribute, dissipate.

Interdependence → Feedback

Dependent origination and control: balancing and reinforcing loops write experience.

Balance → Oscillation

Yin–yang, Aristotle’s mean: coherence as motion that doesn’t tear itself apart.

Time → Impulse Responses

Return curves after disturbance—snap back, ring and settle, or shift to a new basin.

Selfhood → Pattern

The whirlpool image: identity as structure-through-change.

Ethics → Feasible Region

Dharma, phronesis: constraints that keep motion humane.

Attention → Measurement

Calibrate the lens; what we sample becomes model and movement.

Choke & Leverage

A descriptive lens for where motion jams and where a light touch can travel far.

Choke (System Jam)

  • Energy: budget shortfalls; dispersion outruns capture.
  • Space: feedback ignored; contexts misfit tasks.
  • Time: reinforcing spirals saturate; return delayed.

Leverage (Small → Large)

  • Energy: re‑prioritize capture/throughput over hoarding.
  • Space: tweak constraints/affordances to re‑enable balance.
  • Time: seed a pattern that compounds in the desired direction.
